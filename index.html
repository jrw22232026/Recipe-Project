Chai-Spiced Pumpkin Cookies with Salted Maple Caramel

These chewy, flavor-packed cookies encapsulate the best flavors of fall! Sweet pumpkin coalesces with warming chai spices and a rich, buttery maple caramel to create a unique treat that sounds more complex than it actually is to make. The cookies are sure to be an instant hit with at your next autumn bake sale, tea party, or family gathering!

Photo credit:Ice Cream Inspiration

Ingredients

  • 1.5 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1/4 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/4 tsp grated nutmeg
  • 1/2 cup or 1 stick unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup packed dark brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 1/3 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1.5 cups of all-purpose flour
  • 1.2 tsp. of baking soda
  • 1/2 cup pure maple syrup
  • 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt

Instructions

  1. Whisk the maple syrup and brown sugar in a small saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Whisk at a gentle simmer until it thickens (3 - 4 minutes).
  3. Remove from heat and slowly whisk in the heavy cream and butter until fully incorporated.
  4. Stir in the flaky sea salt. Set the caramel aside to cool and thicken.
  5. Spread the pumpkin purée onto a plate and gently press a paper towel over it to absorb the excess moisture.
  6. In a large bowl, whisk the melted butter, dark brown sugar, and granulated sugar until smooth. Add the blotted pumpkin purée, egg yolk, and vanilla extract, whisking until well combined.
  7. In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, kosher salt, and the entire chai spice blend (cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and nutmeg).
  8. Gradually fold the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients using a rubber spatula until just combined.
  9. Cover the bowl and allow the dough to chill in the fridge for 30 minutes.
  10. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two large baking sheets with parchment paper.
  11. Scoop about 1 ½ tablespoons of dough per cookie and roll into balls. Place them 2 inches apart on the prepared baking sheets.
  12. Using the back of a measuring spoon, press a gentle indentation into the center of each dough ball. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, or until the edges are set but the centers are still soft.
  13. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack. Once the cookies have completely cooled, spoon the thickened salted maple caramel into the center well of each cookie.
  14. Finish with an extra sprinkle of flaky sea salt on top of the caramel. Enjoy!
